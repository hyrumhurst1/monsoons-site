Why this page exists

A release on your own domain is worth far more than the same release on a wire

An independent study of AI citations found that press releases distributed through syndication networks accounted for 0.04% of all AI citations, while the same category of content hosted on a company’s own domain accounted for 18.15% of ChatGPT citations. The text is identical. Only the address changes.

The reason is duplication. When a release lands on thirty aggregators at once, an answer engine sees thirty near-identical copies and has no reason to prefer any of them. A single authoritative version on the company’s own site does not have that problem.

So every client we work with gets their coverage published on their own domain first. The wire still goes out afterwards, because it does a different job: it puts the announcement in front of journalists, and it builds the consistent record of independent mentions that teaches an answer engine the business exists.

Source: Search Engine Journal, on the BuzzStream and Citation Labs analysis.
