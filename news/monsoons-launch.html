Mesa Company Monsoons AI Gets East Valley Businesses Named by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity

New Arizona service measures whether ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity recommend a local business, and does not charge the balance until they do.

MESA, Ariz. — When a Mesa resident asks an AI assistant who to call for a repair or where to eat, the answer is usually a short list of about three business names. Monsoons AI, a new company based in Mesa, works to get local businesses onto that list, and measures whether it happened.

The service targets a shift in how people search. Instead of returning ten links for a query like “who should I hire to fix my water heater in Mesa,” assistants such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Perplexity increasingly return a small number of named recommendations with no links at all. A business absent from that answer has no equivalent of a second page to appear on.

“Being fourth on Google was survivable. Being left out of a three-name answer is not. Most owners here have never been shown what AI currently says about their business, and it takes about ten minutes to find out.”
— Hyrum Hurst, founder, Monsoons AI

Monsoons AI serves Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Tempe, Queen Creek and Apache Junction. Its focus is storefront and service businesses: restaurants, cafes, salons, dentists, auto repair shops, gyms, and home-service trades including plumbing, HVAC, landscaping and junk removal.

The method

Five questions, ten runs each, three assistants

The company’s work has two parts. It publishes factual written coverage of a business across independent websites and makes the business’s details consistent everywhere an answer engine checks, including its Google Business Profile. It then measures the result against a baseline recorded before any work began.

The measurement is deliberately repetitive, because answer engines do not return the same answer twice. Monsoons runs five agreed questions, ten times each, across three assistants — 150 individual measurements per campaign — and compares them against the same 150 measurements taken before work started.

A business owner’s own ChatGPT account is personalised to them and returns different results from a clean session, so all measurements are run from fresh sessions with no account history.

The guarantee

The balance is owed only when the standard is met

The measurement standard is set in writing before work starts. The remaining balance is owed when three of the five agreed questions show the business cited or recommended in at least five of ten fresh runs, on at least two of three named assistants, within 30 days.

A miss means a second campaign at the company’s cost. A second miss means the balance is refunded. The full standard is published at monsoonsai.com.

What is not promised

Three things the company says it cannot do

Monsoons AI publishes what it will not promise alongside what it will. The company states that it cannot promise a first-place position, because answer engines do not return ranked lists; that visibility gained from any single campaign fades as competitors publish; and that it does not guarantee a number of leads or a revenue figure.

“We would rather explain the part that fades on day one than have a client discover it in month three.”
— Hyrum Hurst

The company offers a free initial audit, in which it runs five questions a business’s own customers would plausibly ask across three assistants and returns the screenshots, including the names the assistants give instead of the client’s.

Common questions

What business owners ask about AI search

Why isn’t my business showing up in ChatGPT?

Usually because the information an answer engine can find about you is thin or inconsistent. Engines build a picture of a business from many independent sources. If those sources disagree on your name, phone or services, or barely exist, you get left out of the answer.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

Make the same facts about your business true in enough independent places that the engine treats them as settled: a complete Google Business Profile, consistent details everywhere they appear, published coverage on sites the engine already reads, and recent reviews.

What is answer engine optimization?

Work aimed at getting a business named inside an AI assistant’s answer, rather than ranked in a list of links. Assistants return a few names and no page two, so the goal shifts from placement to inclusion.

Is this different from SEO?

Yes. SEO is about where you land in a list of links. This is about whether you get named in an answer that has no links in it. Different mechanism, different work, and it moves faster. They do not conflict.

Does my restaurant or shop actually need to worry about this?

It depends whether your customers ask AI assistants for recommendations yet. The way to find out is to run the questions your customers would ask and see who gets named. Monsoons does that free.

The team

Built by the generation that searches this way

Monsoons AI is deliberately built from the demographic driving the change it sells against. Asking an assistant who to hire, rather than searching and comparing links, skews young, and the team searches that way itself.

“Nobody has ten years of experience in this, because answer engines have not existed for ten years. Everyone in this category is new. The question is who understands how people are searching now, and that is not a question experience answers.”
— Hyrum Hurst

The team combines a working business owner, a commission salesperson who sells to local businesses for a living, a self-taught developer who builds the measurement tooling, and a content creator — a deliberately different mix from a marketing agency, and all from the East Valley.

Monsoons trains its own people rather than hiring credentialed marketers, on the view that the method is teachable and the measurement is what matters. The company publishes the standard it measures against before a client pays, and refunds the balance when it misses.
