How it works

Five steps, thirty days, nothing hidden

  1. We run the questions your customers actually ask

    On Gemini, ChatGPT and Perplexity. We save exactly what comes back before we touch anything. That’s your baseline, and it’s the whole reason we can prove what changed.

  2. We agree on five questions, in writing

    We propose them, you approve them. Once they’re signed the list is frozen, so nobody moves the goalposts, including us.

  3. We go to work

    We write up your business, what you do, where you work, what’s new, and get it published across news and industry sites. Then we make sure your name, address and hours match everywhere AI checks, because inconsistent information is a reason to leave you out.

  4. We measure the same five questions

    Ten fresh runs each, on all three platforms, screenshotted and dated. We measure across ten because these systems don’t answer the same way twice, one lucky screenshot proves nothing and we won’t pretend it does.

  5. Then you pay the rest

    Only if it worked, by the standard we agreed to in step two.

What we won’t promise you

Everyone in this business is about to start overpromising. Here’s our list up front so you can hold us to it.

  • We won’t promise you’ll be number one. AI answers aren’t ranked. We work on getting you named at all.
  • We won’t promise it’s permanent. The bump fades. What stays is a bigger, more consistent record of your business, which is why people keep it going.
  • We won’t promise the AI will “just know you.” That would mean getting into how the model was built, and nobody can sell you that.
  • We won’t promise a number of leads or a dollar figure. We guarantee you show up in the answer. What you do on the phone after that is your business.
  • We won’t promise an exact date. Most clients see it move within a day, and that is genuinely what usually happens, but indexing is not something we control. So we guarantee thirty days and let the fast part be a surprise.
  • We won’t write anything about you that isn’t true. No invented awards, no “best in Arizona.” Wire services reject it and false claims land on your company, not ours.

If you’ve been pitched by someone who promised any of the above, that’s worth knowing about them.

Questions

The ones we get asked

Does anybody actually use AI to find a contractor?

Not everybody, yet. It skews younger, which is also who’s buying houses in Gilbert and Queen Creek right now. The cost of being early on this is one campaign. The cost of being late is that a competitor is the name it gives for the next couple of years.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO is about where you land in a list of links. This is about whether you get named in an answer that has no links in it. Different mechanism, different work, and it moves a lot faster. They don’t conflict, if you have someone doing SEO, keep them.

What if my competitor does this too?

Then it comes down to who has more out there, which is why starting now matters. Right now almost nobody in the East Valley is doing it, so you’re not competing for it. That won’t be true in a year.

I already have a marketing guy. Do I fire him?

No. Ask him what he’s doing about AI answers. If he’s on it, you don’t need us. If he looks blank, that’s the gap we fill, and we’ll work alongside him, not against him.

Why do you measure ten times instead of just showing me?

Because these systems genuinely don’t answer the same way twice, and your own ChatGPT knows you and remembers your chats, so it’ll show you something different than a clean test does. “Named in six of ten runs, up from zero” is a real result. One screenshot is a coin flip.

What do you need from me?

About twenty minutes. A short form covering what you do, where you work, anything new going on, your licenses and any real awards, and a few photos. We write everything from that and you approve it before it goes anywhere.

Who owns the coverage you write?

You do. It’s about your business, it stays published whether you keep working with us or not, and you can post and reuse it however you like.

You’re a new company. Why should I trust you?

Don’t. That is what the deposit structure is for: you are out a quarter of it until we have actually put you in the answer, and we show you your own phone instead of a slide deck. We would rather earn it than claim it. More about who we are.
