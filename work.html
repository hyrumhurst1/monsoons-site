What we are not going to tell you

  • This is not a salary. You get paid when work gets done. Some weeks that is nothing.
  • You get paid when we get paid. Part when the client’s deposit lands, the rest when the balance clears. If a client refunds, the commission reverses. We would rather say that now than surprise you with it.
  • Most doors say no. Talking to the owner at one door in three is a good day, not a bad one.
  • You are a contractor, not an employee. You invoice us, we file a 1099. That means real income you can show a landlord, and it also means taxes are yours to handle.
  • There is no cap and no floor. Nobody is going to hand you leads.
